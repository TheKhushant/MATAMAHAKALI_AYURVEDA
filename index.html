About Our College

Established with a vision to revive and propagate the ancient science of Ayurveda through quality education, research, and patient care.

Mata Mahakali College of Ayurveda & Hospital, Warora

Located in the serene town of Warora, Chandrapur district, our institution is dedicated to producing competent Ayurvedic practitioners who can serve society with holistic healthcare. The college is affiliated with Maharashtra University of Health Sciences (MUHS), Nashik, and recognized by the National Commission for Indian System of Medicine (NCISM).

Our campus features well-equipped classrooms, advanced laboratories, a rich herbal garden, a library with rare Ayurvedic texts, and a 200-bedded hospital for hands-on clinical training. We believe in nurturing the physical, mental, and spiritual well-being of our students.

Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine & Surgery

BAMS

BAMS is a 5.5-year undergraduate degree (including 1 year internship) that integrates modern medical sciences with the ancient wisdom of Ayurveda. The program is recognized by NCISM and affiliated to Maharashtra University of Health Sciences (MUHS).
